The questions buyers actually ask

Can I buy from abroad without flying in?

Yes. Most of our diaspora clients handle everything remotely, from plot selection through allocation, documentation and payment, then inspect on their next trip home. We can also do a video walkthrough of your specific plot.

What exactly is the title?

Plots sit within the Lekki Free Zone and are issued with registered NEPZA title documentation, backed by a governed masterplan. Ask for the full documentation pack and have your own lawyer review it. We would rather you did.

Am I forced to build immediately?

No. Development guidelines and timelines apply and are shared in writing before you commit, so you know exactly what you are agreeing to.

Is Alárò City actually built, or still on paper?

Built and building. Roads, power, water and drainage are in, and factories in the industrial zone are operating. Wellington College International is under construction on site and opens in September 2027. The homes and apartments shown on this page are renderings. The Universal One apartments are sold off-plan and complete in July 2029.

Can I resell before I finish paying?

Resale and transfer are possible under the stated terms. Most investors hold through the infrastructure build-out, which is where the appreciation has historically been. We will go through the specifics with you on a call.

Why is it priced in dollars?

Because a dollar-denominated asset holds its value through naira swings. It is one of the main reasons diaspora buyers pick Alárò over naira-priced estates.
